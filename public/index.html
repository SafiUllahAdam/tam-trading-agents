My 3 rules

1
Time. Short-chart trades (M5–M30) closed by 22:00 Paris. Everything closed by Friday 21:00.
Late evening and weekends: thin market, wide spreads, gaps through stops.
2
Losses. 1 stop-loss → 60-min break. 2 stop-losses → done for the day. No two trades on the same currency. Never bigger size after a loss.
Stops revenge trading and doubling risk on one idea.
3
Size. Risk % from the table (max 2%, D1 only). Max 3% open at once. Take-profit at least 1 × stop-loss (1:1).
The stop distance decides the lot size — never the other way round.

Two chart views

Heikin Ashi (smoothed candles) → only for direction: colour and Alligator.

Normal candles → for all prices: entry, stop-loss, take-profit, levels.

Bigger chart gives the direction. Entry chart gives the moment. Never change chart in the middle of a trade.

How the three agents work

1
Scout reads the live charts once a minute: candles, Heikin Ashi colour, Alligator, peaks and dips, currency strength and red news.
Free delayed-by-seconds feed. Prices can differ slightly from your MT4 broker.
2
Strategist applies the checklist to every instrument: bigger chart direction, price structure, entry chart colour, candle close past the last peak or dip, stop beyond the last dip or peak.
It only says "ready" on the first finished candle past the level. Two candles past = missed.
3
Risk Guard checks the setup against My 3 rules, the session, strong vs weak, red news and your open trades, then sizes the lot from your balance.
It runs in this browser. Balance, stop-loss count and trades are never uploaded.

The agents suggest. You decide, on your own chart, and only when the last checklist step (Calm) is true.
